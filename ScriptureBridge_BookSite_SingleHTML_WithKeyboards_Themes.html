Welcome — ようこそ

Seven chapters, one bridge: bilingual scripture study (EN/JA), speaking practice, brush calligraphy, and a Japan map journal. Everything saves locally on your device.

Quick Start (tap to collapse)

1) Go to Study and create a card.
2) Tap Keyboard (bottom) to type with EN or かな.
3) Practice: Speak + Record.
4) Try Calligraphy guides (kana + common words).
5) Drop travel pins on Japan and attach photos/notes.
6) Back up via Progress → Export.

On-screen Keyboards

Tap a text field, then tap Keyboard at the bottom. Choose EN or かな (hiragana) / カナ (katakana).

Local-first Storage

Cards and phrases use LocalStorage. Pins and media (audio/video/photos) use IndexedDB for persistence.
